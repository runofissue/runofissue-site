For sponsors

Reach the people who make ad tech, media and marketing decisions.

Independent newsletters and podcasts are where senior operators go to make sense of the industry. We help you show up there, alongside writers your buyers already read and trust.

One contact

Work with one person to plan and book your placements, instead of chasing each writer separately.

Simple booking

Tell us who you want to reach and when. We come back with options, dates and pricing, and handle the logistics through to send.

The publisher approves every placement

Each publication signs off on every sponsor and every line of copy, which is part of why their readers trust what they read.

Clearly labeled

Sponsored content is always marked as sponsored. No disguised ads, no surprises for readers or for you.

For publishers

You write. We find the sponsors.

Selling sponsorships takes time most independent publishers would rather spend on the work itself. We act as your outbound sales team, on your terms.

We find and pitch new sponsors

We research companies that fit your readers and reach out to them directly, so new demand comes in without you having to sell.

You approve everything

Every advertiser, every price and every line of copy goes past you first. Nothing runs in your publication without your yes.

Your existing partners stay yours

Sponsors you already work with remain your relationships. We focus on bringing in new ones.

No upfront cost

We work on commission only. If we don't bring you a sponsor, you don't pay anything.

About

Built by someone from the sell side.

Run of Issue was founded by Aaron Palley, whose background is the sell side of programmatic advertising: working with publishers, Prebid, SSPs and DSPs. That means we speak the language of the readers these newsletters serve, and of the sponsors trying to reach them.

We use AI for the research and legwork behind outreach, which lets a small team give each publication and each sponsor proper attention.
